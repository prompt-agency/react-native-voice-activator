# Professional Services

Need a custom wake word or branded voice for your app — but don't want to deal with Icefall, Piper, ONNX exports, and mobile integration yourself?

I help teams ship production-ready custom voice models with this package.

## What I can help with

**Custom wake word training**
Train a Sherpa-ONNX KWS model for your phrase ("Hey Brand", "OK App", etc.), export the bundle, and wire it into your React Native app. Covers data collection guidance, training, ONNX export, and integration with `engineConfig.assetKeys`.

**Custom TTS voice**
Fine-tune a Piper ONNX voice on your brand voice or character, export and quantize for mobile, and integrate with `CustomTTSAdapter`. Covers recording hygiene, training, export, phonemizer wiring, and on-device testing.

**Integration help**
Stuck on Expo prebuild asset bundling, iOS/Android native setup, or the session API? I can debug or pair on your specific setup.
